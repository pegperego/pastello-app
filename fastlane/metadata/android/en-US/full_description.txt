Pastello is a drawing book for children: tap the "+" and draw. No complicated menus, no ads and no internet.

<b>To draw</b>
• Pencil, marker, brush, crayon and rainbow, in four widths
• An eraser that removes only the color and keeps the paper
• 12 colors always at hand and 40 more to choose from
• Ready-made shapes: circle, square, triangle, star, heart, line and arrow
• Colored, lined, squared or dotted paper
• Undo and redo, even after closing the app

<b>To write and decorate</b>
• Text with fonts made for children learning to read, cursive included
• More than 200 stickers: animals, food, nature, vehicles, parties and more
• Move, resize, rotate and duplicate anything, even several things at once with the lasso

<b>Everything safe</b>
• Drawings are saved automatically
• The trash keeps deleted drawings for 30 days
• Save to the gallery and print; sharing first asks for a small grown-ups check
• A parents' page, behind an addition: language, left-handed layout, sounds and vibration

<b>Privacy</b>
Pastello collects no data at all and does not even ask for internet access. No ads, no purchases, no accounts.

<b>Open source</b>
Pastello is free software (GPL-3.0): its code is open to everyone at https://github.com/pegperego/pastello-app
